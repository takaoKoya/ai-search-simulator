## 13.1 The pattern across every row

Every gap above is a **missing layer or a missing feedback loop**, not a broken foundation. The sales pipeline, delivery pipeline, growth-loop measurement, approval routing, RLS/tenancy, and OAuth/document infrastructure are all real and well-built. What is missing is everything that would make the system act *without* a human or an (unwired) cron tick starting each individual step: a Planner, a Scheduler that's actually wired up, an Event bus with subscribers, a Supervisor, Memory/Knowledge, and a company-level Goal hierarchy to drive all of it. This is the basis for `15_TARGET_ARCHITECTURE.md` and the sequencing in `16_MIGRATION_PLAN.md`.
